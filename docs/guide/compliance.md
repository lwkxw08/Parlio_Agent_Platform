## Retention

How long recordings, transcripts and messages are kept before automatic deletion (for example recordings 30 days, transcripts 12 months). **Save policy** applies going forward; **Run sweep now** deletes anything already past its date. *Last sweep* shows what was removed.

## PII redaction

Masks card numbers, sort codes, dates of birth and similar from transcripts and summaries. Payment card details are never stored regardless of this setting.

## Subject access & erasure (UK GDPR)

Search a phone number or email. **Export** produces a JSON bundle of everything held about that person (calls, transcripts, messages, tickets, bookings, contact record) to answer a subject access request. **Erase** deletes it and issues an erasure receipt you can keep as evidence; the audit log keeps only the fact that an erasure happened.

## Audit log

Every configuration change, data export, erasure, login and admin action, with who, when and from where. Use **Refresh** to update. The log is append-only.

The **Compliance pack** (DPA, sub-processor list, security overview) is under *Settings*.
