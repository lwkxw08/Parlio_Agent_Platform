## Contacts list

Everyone who has called, texted or chatted, matched by phone number, with name, status, first and last contact, call count and lifetime value. **Search** by name or number. Open a contact to see their calls, tickets, bookings and messages, add notes, or change their status.

## Status

- **Prospect** – default for new callers.
- **Customer** – set automatically when a booking is completed, a payment is taken or a job ticket is resolved (or when a connected CRM says so), or set by hand.
- **VIP** – by the rules below, from a connected CRM, or by hand.
- **Blocked** – calls from this contact are ended without answering. This is the easiest way to block a specific person; for anonymous callers use *Assistant Studio → Call screening*.

A status you set manually is pinned: the automatic rules will not change it again.

## How callers are classified

- **VIP after this many calls** and **VIP once lifetime value reaches (£)** – automatic promotion thresholds.
- **Route VIPs to department** – VIP callers who ask for a person go straight to this department.
- **Named VIP accounts** – names or numbers, one per line, that are always VIP.
- **How the assistant treats VIPs** / **existing customers** – instructions added to the assistant's context when it recognises the caller ("always offer a same-day visit", "never ask for the account number again").

The assistant is told the caller's name, status and last-call summary at the start of each call, so it can greet a returning customer by name and follow these rules. **Save rules** applies immediately (no publish needed).
