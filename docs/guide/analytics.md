## Ask AI

Type a question — "how many calls did we miss after 5pm last month?", "which department gets the most calls on Mondays?" — and the page filters itself and answers. The chips underneath are common questions.

## Overview

Choose the **period** and optional filters (business hours / after hours, weekdays / weekends, department, assistant, location).

- **Call volume** – calls per day as a smooth curve; the shaded area is total, the line is answered.
- **Day of week** and **Volume by hour** – when your callers ring, to plan staffing and hours.
- **Calls by department** – donut of what callers wanted.
- **First-time vs returning** – new callers versus known contacts.
- **Handoff** – transfers, tickets and how quickly they were answered.
- **Usage this month** – calls, minutes, transfers, tickets, bookings, SMS, WhatsApp, web chats and emails against your plan allowance.
- **Call / Transfer / Prospect metrics** – the underlying numbers.

## Compare

Switch to **Compare** to see this period against the previous one (or the same period last year): daily trend comparison, day-of-week comparison and the metric deltas.

## Insights

Deeper read-outs over 30 days to 2 years:

- **Demand** – hour × weekday heat-map, a next-week forecast, and "calls we'd have missed" by hour if the assistant weren't answering.
- **Trends** – volume, answer rate, booking rate and QA score over time.
- **Resolution** – the funnel from call to resolved (answered → need identified → resolved by AI / transferred / ticket), SLA attainment and transfer quality.
- **Team** – per-department and per-person answered rates, talk time and time to answer.
- **Customers** – new vs returning, repeat callers, VIPs, unanswered-question gaps.
- **Revenue** – bookings and qualified leads valued with your *Value → Business assumptions*, and attribution by channel.

## AI business advisor

Every week the advisor reads the Insights and writes evidence-backed recommendations ("Tuesdays 4–6pm are your busiest unanswered hour — add an on-call destination", "12 callers asked about boiler servicing, which isn't in your FAQs"). For each: **Apply** (where it can change a setting for you), **Dismiss**, or **Snooze**. **Advisor settings** control the digest day/hour and how far back it looks.

## Reports & export

**Export CSV** downloads any of the Insights tables for the period. **Scheduled report** emails a summary weekly or monthly on the day and hour you choose; past deliveries are listed under *Sent reports*.
