## Board

Tickets are created when a caller asks for a call back, a transfer isn't answered, an urgent keyword is heard, a customer replies "2" to a reminder, or a web-chat visitor asks for a human. Columns: **New**, **Claimed**, **Resolved**. Each card shows the caller, department, priority, what they need (the collected details) and the SLA countdown from *Transfers* settings; overdue tickets are highlighted and trigger the "ticket overdue" notification rule.

## Working a ticket

- **Claim** it so colleagues know it's yours; **Resolve** when done (with an outcome note).
- **Call back with AI** – hands the ticket to the assistant to ring the customer with the resolution you type in (it never re-collects details or opens a second ticket). Requires the *Outbound* calling policy to allow it. **Dial now** ignores the calling window and daily cap; do-not-call still applies.
- **Open conversation** – when the ticket came from a chat or SMS thread, it links to the Inbox thread; resolving either side updates the other.
- **Add note** – internal notes visible to your team only.

Resolving a ticket that relates to a job promotes the contact from Prospect to Customer (see *Contacts*).
