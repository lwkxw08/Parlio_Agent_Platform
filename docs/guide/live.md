## Live calls

Calls in progress right now with a streaming transcript. Select one to:

- **Listen** – hear the call in your browser without either side knowing.
- **Whisper** – type an instruction the assistant follows immediately ("offer Thursday at 2pm", "tell them Dave is on his way") — the caller doesn't hear the whisper.
- **Say** – have the assistant speak a sentence verbatim.
- **Take over** – you speak to the caller directly through your browser; the assistant goes quiet. **Hand back** returns control; **Hang up** ends the call.

## Approvals

When a rule requires permission before the assistant does something (a refund, a discount, a same-day appointment) it asks the caller to hold and posts a request here — and, if configured, to Slack or SMS with a tap-to-approve link. **Approve** or **Decline** with an optional note; the assistant tells the caller the outcome. Requests time out after the period set in the rule and the assistant takes a message instead.
