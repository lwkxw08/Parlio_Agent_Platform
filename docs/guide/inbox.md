## Conversations

Threads from every text channel. Filter by **Open**, **Waiting on us**, **Closed** or **Unassigned**. The AI answers first (using your FAQs and rules) and hands off to a person when the visitor asks or when it can't help; a handoff shows a badge on the sidebar and, if configured, a Slack/SMS alert. In a thread you can **Reply**, **Pause AI** so it stops answering while you handle it, **Assign** to a teammate, add internal **Notes**, and **Close**. A callback ticket linked to the thread progresses with it.

## Canned replies

Saved responses with a **Shortcut** (type `/` in the reply box). Use them for opening hours, directions, price lists.

## Web chat widget

Adds a chat bubble to your website. Set the **Title**, **Accent colour**, **Greeting** and **Allowed website origins** (the domains the widget may load on), then copy the **Embed snippet** into your site. Visitors can also click to talk — a browser voice call to the same assistant. Chats appear here as threads.

## WhatsApp Business

Connect a WhatsApp Business (Cloud API) number with its **Phone number ID**, **Display number** and **Permanent access token** from Meta Business Manager. Incoming WhatsApp messages become threads; the assistant replies within WhatsApp's rules.

## SMS

Texts to your ParlioTec SMS number arrive as threads automatically; no setup beyond having an SMS-capable number on *Billing*. Replies to appointment reminders (1, 2, STOP) are handled automatically and also shown here.
