## Leads

**Add a lead** by hand (name, phone in international format, email, what they're interested in) or feed them in automatically from a **Web form** on any website or via **Zapier / Make / webhooks**. A new lead is called within the **Speed-to-lead target** (default 60 seconds) inside the calling window. **Do not call** suppresses a lead permanently.

## Queue and history

**Queued** shows scheduled calls with their next attempt time; **All history** shows outcomes (answered, voicemail, no answer, busy, completed) and links to the call. **Schedule a call** creates a one-off call with a **Purpose** (call back, reminder, review request, custom) and an optional time. **Dial now** places a call immediately even outside the window or past the daily cap — do-not-call still applies.

## Calling policy

Keeps you compliant with Ofcom/TPS rules:

- **Jurisdiction preset** (UK by default), **Time zone** and **Calling window** – no calls outside these hours.
- **Max attempts per call**, **Gap between attempts** and **Daily cap per number** – limit how often one person is tried.
- **Speed-to-lead target**, **Reminder (hours before booking)**, **Review request (hours after)** – timing for the automatic purposes.
- **Caller ID shown** – must be one of your numbers.
- **Opt-out phrases** – if the person says any of these, the number is added to do-not-call.
- **What the assistant may call about** – tick the purposes you allow. Ticket call-backs are only made when a person has chosen "Call back with AI" on the ticket.

## Do-not-call list

Numbers that will never be dialled. Customers are added automatically when they ask not to be called or text STOP; add or remove by hand here. TPS-registered numbers are screened when the jurisdiction preset is UK.
