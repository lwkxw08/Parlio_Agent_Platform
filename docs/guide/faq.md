## Can I block anonymous or withheld callers?

Yes. *Assistant Studio → Call screening*: tick **Reject withheld / anonymous caller IDs**, then **Publish**. It is off by default because many genuine customers withhold their number. To screen rather than reject, set **Screen** to *Unknown callers only* — the assistant asks who they are and why they're calling first. To block one specific number, add it under **Blocked numbers** in the same section, or set that contact's status to *Blocked* on *Contacts*.

## How do I stop spam and sales calls?

*Assistant Studio → Call screening*: tick **Reject numbers on your spam list and the platform-wide spam list**, and set **Screen** to *Unknown callers only*. Robocalls, silent lines and sales pitches are ended politely and shown on *Calls* as "Screened".

## How do I change the greeting or what the assistant says?

*Assistant Studio*: greeting, tone and business name are at the top; rules under **Key business rules**; answers under **FAQs**. Press **Publish** — changes to a draft don't affect live calls until then.

## How do I change the voice?

*Assistant Studio → Voice*: pick from the accent-grouped catalogue with previews, then **Publish**. Your own cloned voice is set up on *Quality & simulate → Owner voice cloning*.

## How do I set opening hours and holidays?

*Assistant Studio → Business hours*, with **Holidays & closures** for specific dates. What callers hear when closed is under **Closed-hours persona**.

## How do I get a text after each call?

*Integrations → Notifications → Text me after every call*: enter your mobile and **Turn on summaries**.

## How do I send appointment reminders?

Connect a calendar (*Integrations → Calendar → Connect*), then in the same tab scroll to **SMS appointment reminders**, enable, set the offsets (e.g. 24h and 1h) and **Save**. Customers reply 1 to confirm or 2 to reschedule.

## How do I connect Google Calendar or Outlook?

*Integrations → Calendar → Connect*: choose Google Calendar or Outlook / Microsoft 365 and sign in with the account whose diary should be used.

## How do I transfer calls to my mobile or a colleague?

*Transfers → Departments & destinations → + Add person*: type *Phone number*, enter it in international format, set hours and priority. Choose warm or cold transfer in *Assistant Studio → No-answer behaviour*.

## What happens if nobody answers a transfer?

*Assistant Studio → No-answer behaviour*: take a message as a ticket (default), offer voicemail, or both. Tickets appear on *Tickets & callbacks*.

## How do I stop recording calls?

*Assistant Studio → Recording & consent*: switch **Record calls** off and **Publish**. Existing recordings follow the retention policy on *Compliance*.

## How do I add website chat or WhatsApp?

*Inbox → Channels*: **Web chat widget** gives you an embed snippet; **WhatsApp Business** takes your Meta Cloud API details.

## How do I export or delete a customer's data?

*Compliance → Subject access & erasure*: search the number or email, then **Export** or **Erase**.

## How do I add a colleague?

*Team → Invite a teammate*, choose a role (Owner, Admin, Agent, Viewer).

## How do I change my plan or add a number?

*Billing & usage*: **Change plan** under Current subscription; **Add a UK number** at the bottom.

## Why is there a test call I didn't make?

ParlioTec places one automatic synthetic test call a day to check your assistant is working. It appears on *Health → Synthetic call history*, not on your Calls page.
