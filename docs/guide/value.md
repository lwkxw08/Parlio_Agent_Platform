## Business value

Headline cards: estimated revenue from bookings and qualified leads this period, revenue you would have missed without the assistant (calls answered outside hours or while your line was busy), and what callers wanted.

## Top leads

Callers scored as likely buyers, with the reason (asked for a quote, gave an address, urgent need) and a link to the call and contact.

## Tracking numbers

Give each marketing channel its own number so you know what's working. **Add a tracking number**: the number, the **Channel** (Google Ads, website, van livery, Checkatrade…), an optional **Campaign** and **Monthly spend**. *By marketing channel* then shows calls, leads and revenue per channel and cost per lead.

## Weekly owner digest

A short email every week: calls, leads, bookings, revenue estimate, misses and one thing to fix. Choose the **Day** and **Hour**; **Send digest now** sends one immediately.

## Business assumptions

The numbers behind the estimates: **Average job value**, **Value of a booking**, the percentage of **Qualified leads that become a sale**, and the percentage of **Missed calls that were a lead**. Set these honestly and the revenue figures across Value and Analytics → Revenue become meaningful.
